## A. 5:27–28
- Question (open, answered in this group): what 「凡看見婦女就動淫念」 means [S0002–S0003].
- Position: seeing someone as attractive is already adultery in the heart (many Christians) [S0002]. Refuted by the Claim below.
- 「那種禱告，神不會聽」 [S0003]: lays out the problem; part of the Question step (no reason is given here).
- Evidence: πρὸς τὸ + infinitive expresses purpose, not result [S0004, S0006]. The falling and face-rash examples illustrate purpose vs result → part of that Evidence. The lunch-and-spicy-food account [S0004 tail–S0005] is the story inside that illustration, so it stays with it (owner: a supporting story keeps its whole length).
- Evidence: Matt 13:17 ἐπιθυμέω vs Luke 10:24 θέλω for the same saying → ἐπιθυμέω includes the will [S0006, S0008–S0009]. One comparison.
- Inside it, the Amen aside [S0006 tail–S0008 head]: Jesus puts Amen first, replacing 「神的話臨到我」 → Jesus speaks with God's authority. This is its own Claim with its own reasoning (not about 5:28). 「很多人說耶穌沒有講祂是神…很多人就沒有眼睛看見」 is a Position refuted by it, not a 題外話 (reasoning is given right before).
- Evidence: 1 Pet 4:2–3 (ἐπιθυμία set against θέλημα) and Eph 2:3 → desire includes the will [S0031].
- Claim (centre): Jesus means deciding by the will to act with her; at that point one has sinned, before the act; finding her attractive is not sin [S0009 end, S0032, S0036].
- Evidence: James 1:14–15 — desire conceives, then gives birth to sin → desire alone is not yet sin [S0033]. The Lexus example illustrates the same step [S0034].
- Claim: James uses ἐπιθυμία for desire alone; the other NT writers use it for desire plus will. Same as faith: James calls A (accepting revealed truth on sufficient evidence) 信 and B 行為; Paul and John call A+B 信 → the words differ, not the teaching [S0035]. The faith comparison is Evidence for this Claim.
- Claim (application): desire is temptation, not sin, but staying with it leads to sin; flee and turn the mind elsewhere [S0036].
