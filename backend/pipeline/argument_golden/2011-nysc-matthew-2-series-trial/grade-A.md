## Recurring failure patterns

**P1. A general principle drawn from cases is absorbed under the exegesis of one verse instead of becoming a top-level point** (G07: K2, sin is the will's decision).
- The rule exists: 「他在案例後以「所以……」得出的一般原則（讀經、倫理或教義）是本組結論之一，案例支持它，不掛在案例底下」.
- But it is triggered by surface form, "after the case, with 「所以」". Here he states the principle in the middle, as a rhetorical question (「犯罪的很重要的意思是什麼？」), between cases. *The prompt fails to prevent this*, because the sentence is tied to a word and a position rather than to scope: a judgment that applies beyond the passage being explained.

**P2. One Claim carries several separately judgeable points** (CL35, CL36, CL37, CL64, and CL22 in part).
- The rules are clear: 「一條Claim只含一個可單獨判斷對錯的判斷」 and 「他接著逐一講解的部分（給了說明或理由），各成一條分論點」.
- Mostly an *execution slip*. However, the neighbouring exception 「描述同一結構的幾句算一個」 and 「同一要點的不同說法不另立」 give the model a ready excuse to bundle a judgment, its limit and its consequence into one line. This happens most where he says "but this does not mean…", as in CL37.

**P3. A premise or explanatory sub-point is raised to top level** (CL55 in G20, CL39 in G05).
- Prompt text that invites it: 「兩個互不從屬的結論各為頂層，不硬合成一條」.
- The prompt says where a *reused* premise goes (「沿用的那條作它的前提」) but not where a *local* premise goes: the conclusion that creates the question this group answers. A premise that carries its own judgment then looks like a second independent conclusion. *The prompt fails to prevent this.*

**P4. An illustration or aside becomes a Claim** (CL47, the faith analogy; CL54, "evidence matters").
- CL47 is an *execution slip* despite 「比喻即使帶有教義內容仍是例子」. The analogy has doctrinal content, so it was carded.
- CL54 is *caused by the prompt*. 「但他明白教導、並在本組用來得出要點的讀經原則，是提綱上的點」 promotes any methodological remark. The opposite rule, 「不把口頭強調、重複或未展開預告獨立建卡」, is not weighed against it. The prompt does not say that a reading principle must actually be used to derive a point *here*, and is not a transition into the next piece of evidence.

**P5. A top-level statement restates a sub-point that has its own Claim** (CL22 restates CL26; CL29 partly restates CL38).
- The rule is clear: 「已各成分論點的部分，上層只點出名稱，不重述其內容」. *Execution slip.*

**P6. Rebuttals of an opposing reading are typed `qualifies`** (CL15, CL18, CL61).
- *Caused by the prompt.* The CONNECTIONS text gives Claim-to-Claim links only two choices, 「以supports或qualifies連到它直接支持的Claim」, and reserves 「Claim到Position的refutes表示反方」 for Position nodes, which are not created at the Claim stage. A rebuttal therefore has nowhere to go except `qualifies`, which misstates it as a limit on the conclusion.

**P7. Reused digression Claims float with no route** (CL14 and CL15 in G07).
- The prompt requires the reuse (「離題後落回該點的那句結論要沿用」) and forbids hanging it under a local point (「沿用的其他組Claim不掛在本組分論點底下」). It also requires every point to reach the main conclusion (「使每條分論點都有路線通到主要結論」).
- For a digression that is not a premise of this group, these three rules cannot all be met. *The prompt causes this (a gap).*

**P8. Wording drifts when a contrast or an ambiguous line is summarised** (CL46, which reads as excluding Matthew; CL52, which reads 「不是動了淫念」 as "not lust").
- The rule is clear: 「合併句子時不得擴大或轉移主詞；指涉不明就保留不確定，不自行補人物」. *Execution slip.*
- It happens in condensed summaries of "X versus everyone else" contrasts and in spoken Q&A fragments.

**Strong points.** The Claims follow several rules well, and in several places they are better than the key:
- Material (scripture, word data, stories) is left out of the Claim tree.
- An unexpanded preview is not carded.
- Off-topic rulings he gave in the Q&A are carded.
- The tension and his resolution are kept separate (G05).
- Reservations he stated are preserved: 「姦淫另當別論」 in CL57, and the mislabelled speaker in CL51.
