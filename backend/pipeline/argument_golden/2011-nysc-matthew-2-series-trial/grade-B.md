# Grade — claims-B against key-B (G08–G19)

Sources read: `key-B.md`, `claims-B.md`, `prompt-claims.md`, transcripts `groups/08.md`–`19.md` (owned paragraphs). The key was checked against the transcript wherever the two disagreed.

How the counts work:
- **Main**: whether the Claim tree's top matches the key's top-level conclusion(s), graded right / partly / wrong.
- **Coverage**: counts only the key's sub-points (K*n*.*m*). The tops are graded under Main.
  - *covered*: one Claim holds the point.
  - *merged*: the point sits inside a Claim that also carries another point, or a key "point" that is really an example is carried by the Claim it illustrates.
  - *missing*: no Claim has the point.
  - *not upheld*: the transcript does not support the key's point as stated.
- **Extras**: Claims that are not outline points.
- **Wrong links**: wrong parent or wrong direction. Quibbles about the relation type are listed separately.
- **Fidelity**: a Claim adds to, drops from, or shifts what he said.

## Recurring failure patterns

1. **A reused Claim takes the top, and the landing-back sentence is dropped** (G10).
   - The group's own conclusion (CL32) was subordinated to a reused G09 Claim (CL27). The sentence that lands back on the earlier point (K6.3) was treated as a transition and dropped, which cut the group's route to CL23.
   - **Execution slip despite a clear rule:** "本組自己的主要結論仍是頂層，沿用的那條作它的前提" and "離題後落回該點的那句結論要沿用".
   - **The prompt also pulls the other way:** "不把口頭強調、重複……獨立建卡", "只提到名字、不再講述的不沿用", and "單純……講課轉場不當作實質論據" all invite reading a short landing-back sentence as a transition or a mere naming. The prompt needs a test for telling a landing-back conclusion apart from a name-check.

2. **The top becomes a table of contents** (G15 CL59).
   - When the parts *are* the answer, the top names the parts ("有兩個問題") instead of stating the conclusion.
   - **The prompt causes this:** "「分為幾部分」要寫出各部分是什麼……已各成分論點的部分，上層只點出名稱，不重述其內容". Nothing says the top must still state the conclusion that the parts deliver.

3. **Two judgements packed into one Claim** (CL4, CL9, CL13, CL47 and CL51; this is most of the 35 merges that are not just examples).
   - Typical shapes are a conclusion plus the scripture inference that supports it, or a principle plus its application.
   - **Execution slip:** the rule is clear, "一條Claim只含一個可單獨判斷對錯的判斷".
   - **The prompt adds pressure toward packing:** the slide test ("會寫上投影片的點") and "同一要點的不同說法不另立" both favour compact lines, and there is no worked contrast between "one point said twice" and "two points in one sentence".

4. **Unexplained applications linked as `supports`** (CL80, CL53, and the weaker cases CL40 and CL36).
   - A case he only asserts or applies is linked upward as if it argued for the principle.
   - **The prompt causes this.** The CLAIMS section allows such cases ("他只給結論沒給解釋時，照寫結論"), but CONNECTIONS forces a supporting route: "以supports或qualifies連到它直接支持的Claim……使每條分論點都有路線通到主要結論，不只用contextualizes". No relation covers "an application of".

5. **Hedges dropped or added around partial answers** (CL50 drops "不一定"; CL80 adds a denial he never gave).
   - **Execution slip despite a clear rule:** "他只給結論沒給解釋時，照寫結論及他的保留（如「不一定」），不補理由".

6. **A story lesson promoted to a Claim, and overlapping splits** (CL76; CL50 split from CL49; the CL73/CL74 overlap).
   - **Execution slip:** "例子或故事裡的教訓若只是重述提綱上已有的點不另立" and "同一要點的不同說法不另立" both cover it.

7. **Top-level inflation from the "所以 principle" and "answered question" rules** (G09 has five tops, G13 three, G17 two, G18 two).
   - The transcript usually supports these tops (CL29, CL53, CL66, CL78), but answers such as CL28 should hang under the conclusion they apply to.
   - **The prompt partly causes this:** "他在案例後以「所以……」得出的一般原則……是本組結論之一" is unconditional and sits against "通常一到兩條". The attach-under rule ("掛在它所應用或支持的那條下面") is written only for side answers to "算不算罪", so the model does not apply it to other question answers.

8. **Process notes leaking into the Claim file** (the G17 "本組另以 CL66 為前導" note, which is also factually wrong).
   - **Execution slip:** "處置過程只寫本次決定的reason／outcomes".
