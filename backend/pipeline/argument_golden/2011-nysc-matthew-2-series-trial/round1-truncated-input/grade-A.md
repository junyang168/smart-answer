## Recurring failure patterns (prompt-fixable)

1. **Compression into mega-Claims.** Sub-points the lecturer marks out (segments, 「第一／第二」, 「更進一步」, numbered conclusions) get folded into one long statement. Examples: CL1 swallows 11 points; CL5 fuses the Messiah step with the "God" step; CL8, CL26, CL29 and CL44 each absorb their own sub-points. This is the main reason merged (35) is close to covered (46).
   *Fix:* one Claim per step he marks; a parent Claim states the conclusion, not its parts.
2. **His own inferences from the text demoted to material.** When he draws a conclusion from a word or a parallel, the Claims file it as 「論據」 material. Examples: 「這是很好的證據」 (ptōchos); 5:5 ↔ Isa 61:7; James's concession in Acts 15; the faith A/B analogy.
   *Fix:* separate a bare citation or quotation (material) from an inference he draws from the text (a supporting Claim).
3. **Reading-method lessons attached as "supports" to a nearby exegetical Claim.** This happened in 4 of the 4 groups where one appears: CL16, CL22 in G05, CL38, CL22 in G20. The key consistently places them at the top, and the transcripts back the key: he states them as takeaways (「我講這個就是要告訴各位…」).
   *Fix:* a hermeneutical_principle Claim sits at the top level or is linked as "illustrated by", never as support for an exegetical claim.
4. **Top-level Claims left without a route.** A top-level Claim stays orphaned when he explicitly frames it as an instance of, or a step towards, another point. Examples: CL31 vs G06's 「再舉一個例子」; CL45 vs CL39 and CL18; the reused CL18 in G20.
   *Fix:* every top-level Claim must either be the group's conclusion or link to the conclusion it serves; check announced transitions across group boundaries.
5. **Tension encoded as a supporting Claim.** CL19 "supports" its own resolution CL18 while also being recorded as a Question.
   *Fix:* a tension or problem is a Question answered by the resolving Claim, with no support edge.
6. **Reuse drift.** An earlier Claim is reused for a passing mention, or its old wording is reused where the new passage says something different. Examples: CL8 in G07; CL22 in G20 loses 「分辨」 and the obedience clause.
   *Fix:* reuse only when the group argues the point again and the statement still matches; otherwise keep it as material or create a new Claim.
