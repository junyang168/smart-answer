## Recurring failure patterns (prompt-fixable)

1. **Principle hung under its own instance.** When he finishes an exegesis and says 「所以念聖經
   要……」, the general method principle is attached as a child of the exegesis: CL20→CL19,
   CL38→CL37, reused CL1 and CL2→CL40. 4 of 4 wrong links are this one pattern. Prompt fix: a
   general reading/method principle he draws with 「所以」 at the end of a case is a conclusion; the
   case supports it (or both are siblings). A reused top-level Claim is never made the child of a
   group-local Claim.
2. **A ruling given in passing filed as material.** G09 K1.3 (do not marry an unbeliever, with
   reasons) was called 「順帶的勸勉」; G11 K1.4 (入籍宣誓 is not sin, answered as his own question)
   was called an "應用例子". The same filter correctly dropped real restatements (G11 K1.5,
   G12 K1.3, G15 K1.2), so the rule needs one sharper line, not a looser one: if he states a
   judgement and gives a reason, or answers "is this X a sin / allowed?", it is a Claim even when
   it is a digression; if he only repeats a point already made, it is not.
3. **Several argument steps packed into one Claim.** 34 of 75 matched key points are merged,
   e.g. CL19 (three steps: unremarried wife is no adulteress → women had only two ways to live →
   so the husband forces her to remarry), CL30 (four structural facts), CL8, CL6. For a slide
   outline the steps are what the listener follows; one dense Claim hides them. CL13 also merges
   two separate segments (L2-S0016 and L3-S0030). Prompt fix: when he argues in steps
   (「我問各位……所以……所以」), each step he lands on is its own supporting Claim; do not merge
   points from non-adjacent segments into one statement.
4. **Reuse freezes away new content.** CL35 is reused in G17 with its statement unchanged, so
   G17's added point (「信心本身就需要這種計劃」, 神必供給) appears in no statement. Prompt fix: on
   reuse, if the new group adds a distinct proposition, add a new supporting Claim under the
   reused one instead of only extending ranges.
