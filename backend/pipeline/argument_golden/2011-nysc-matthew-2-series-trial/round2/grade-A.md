How I counted:
- **Covered**: a Claim states this key point (it may also carry other text).
- **Merged**: the point appears only inside a Claim whose main judgment is a different key point.
- **Missing**: the point is not in any Claim, and the transcript shows it is a real outline point.
- **Key not upheld**: the key lists a point that the transcript shows is not an outline point (a preview, narrative, or a restatement owned by another group). These are left out of the coverage judgment against the Claims.
- **Extras** count stand-alone Claims that are not outline points. I list separately the parents that repeat their children's content ("overstuffed parent").

## Recurring failure patterns

### 1. The parent repeats its children's content (CL1, CL11, CL25)
A top Claim lists the parts or steps, and child Claims then state each one again. A slide would show the headline once, with the parts as bullets beneath it.

- For structure Claims (CL1), the prompt partly causes this. It says "statement寫出這一點的內容，不只寫題目：「分為幾部分」要寫出各部分是什麼". It limits this with "他接著逐一講解的部分…各成一條分論點，只列出、未講解的部分留在上層那條", but it never says that explained parts must then be left out of the parent. The model obeys both sentences and repeats the content.
- For step and definition Claims (CL11, CL25), it is an execution slip despite a clear rule: "上層那條只寫結論，不把各步塞進去".

### 2. The line between a method definition and a reading principle shifts from group to group (CL19 vs G01; CL53)
The inclusio definition is material in G01 and a Claim in G03. An analogy (the two elements of faith) becomes a Claim. The prompt causes this, because two sentences pull opposite ways:
- "他用來講明或證明要點的經文、觀察、例子、術語或方法的定義…是論據，不是提綱上的點"
- "但他明白教導、並在本組用來得出要點的讀經原則，是提綱上的點"

Nothing says that explaining a structure or method is a definition while "所以讀經要…" is a principle. Nor does anything say that an analogy carrying its own doctrinal content is still an example.

### 3. A translation or proof text becomes a Claim when its inference only repeats the parent (CL37)
The prompt states the rule ("單純的引經、字詞資料、譯法留給論據"), but "他從經文、字詞或對照得出的推論是Claim" lets through a translation whose added inference is the parent's conclusion again. The prompt fails to prevent this: it does not say the inference must be a new judgment. The rest is an execution slip.

### 4. A conclusion derived from an exegesis is hung under it with "supports" (CL54 → CL48; CL53 → CL52)
A general principle the speaker draws from a passage, or an analogy, is attached as if it proved the passage. The prompt has the right rule only for reading principles: "他在案例後以「所以讀經要……」得出的原則是本組結論之一，案例支持它，不掛在案例底下". It says nothing of ethical or doctrinal principles drawn the same way, so the prompt fails to prevent this.

### 5. A premise or setup is promoted to a separate top (CL70)
The prompt is clear: "其下是他為講明它而逐一講出的要點，例如各部分、理由、前提". This is an execution slip. The model chose "兩個互不從屬的結論各為頂層" for a point that only sets up the main question.

### 6. Q&A and narrative wording slips: a speaker added or a correction reframed (CL34, CL59)
The rule is clear: "逐個動作保留原文的主詞和對象…指涉不明就保留不確定，不自行補人物". This is an execution slip. The risk is higher in Q&A, where speaker turns are garbled (L4-S0012).

### 7. One side of an answered tension drops out of the tree (K1.1.2, "到約翰為止")
When he states a tension and then resolves it, the resolution becomes the Claim and the tension becomes a Question. A scripture side he actually interprets ("就結束了") is then filed as "unanswered" and is in no Claim at all. The prompt fails to prevent this. It covers only the unanswered case ("他只指出問題或張力、本組沒有給出解答時，Claim只寫他陳述的雙方及張力本身") and says nothing about where the sides of an answered tension go.
