Counting rules:
- **Coverage** is counted over key points that the transcript supports as outline points. Where the transcript shows the key itself is wrong (an example promoted to a point, or an umbrella the professor never said), that point is excluded from the denominator and listed under "Key errors".
- **Covered** means one Claim (or a Position, for a view he refutes) carries the point. **Merged** means the point exists only inside a Claim that also carries another key point. **Missing** means no Claim carries it.
- **Extras** are counted per Claim, out of 104 newly created Claims (CL1–CL103 plus CL39a). Reused Claims are not counted.

## Recurring failure patterns

**1. Example and story content becomes Claims, or gets welded into Claim statements.**
- Seen in: CL13, CL80, CL95, CL75, and embedded in CL42 and CL79.
- Cause: an execution slip despite a clear rule, 「他用來講明或證明要點的經文、觀察、例子、術語或方法的定義、背景、引述，是論據，不是提綱上的點，留給後續材料。」
- The prompt contributes. It also says 「他從經文、字詞或對照得出的推論是Claim」 and 「他順帶回答……或給出理由的判斷，即使離題也是Claim」, and the model reads the moral of a story ("resign the job", "teaching truth grows the church") as one of those. Nothing tells it that a lesson drawn inside an example, which restates a point already on the outline, is not a new Claim.

**2. The same point is said twice and gets two Claims.**
- Seen in: CL2/CL3, CL12/CL19, CL66/CL67, CL94/CL95. He typically states a point, illustrates it, and restates it at the end of an answer.
- Cause: an execution slip against a clear rule, 「同一要點的不同說法不另立」.
- 「他逐步推論時……每一步落腳的判斷各成一條分論點」 probably pushes the model toward splitting a restatement off as if it were a further step.

**3. Q&A answers become isolated tops.**
- Seen in: G09 has five tops, including CL40 and CL42. Some separate tops are correct: CL89, CL96, CL21.
- Cause: the prompt fails to prevent this. It says 「提綱頂層是他在這裡要聽眾明白的主要結論，通常一到兩條」 and, separately, that a passing answer 「即使離題也是Claim」. It never says where a passing answer attaches, so the model makes each one a top.
- The CONNECTIONS stage then has to produce 「使每條分論點都有路線通到主要結論」 for Claims the CLAIMS stage never placed under a conclusion.

**4. The cross-group reuse rule misfires in both directions.**
- **(a) A short payoff sentence is treated as passing mention.** In G10, the sentence that lands the whole digression back on divorce was dropped. The prompt causes this with 「只順帶提到的不沿用」: the payoff of a digression is usually one short sentence, which looks like a passing mention.
- **(b) The group's own conclusion is hung under another group's Claim.** In G13, CL69 went under G10's CL44, so the answer to the abortion question became a sub-point of another group's principle. The prompt causes this with 「本組另講出的新判斷另立Claim，掛在沿用的那條下面」: it makes no exception for the case where the new judgment is this group's own conclusion and the reused Claim is only its premise.

**5. A principle drawn after a case is still nested under the case.**
- Seen in: CL87 under CL86. CL39 and CL102 were handled correctly.
- Cause: an execution slip against a clear rule, 「他在案例後以『所以讀經要……』得出的原則是本組結論之一，案例支持它，不掛在案例底下。」

**6. Partial answers are not modelled.**
- Seen in: CL64 drops his 「不一定」. G19 marks a Question answered when only a verdict, with no explanation, was given. CL29 handled a partial answer correctly.
- Cause: the prompt fails to prevent this. It covers only two cases: no answer (「他只指出問題或張力、本組沒有給出解答時，Claim只寫他陳述的雙方及張力本身」) and a full answer. It says nothing about a verdict given without its explanation. The model then either trims the verdict or treats the question as fully answered.

**7. Speaker drift inside quoted dialogue.**
- Seen in: CL13 records a former student's line as the professor's teaching. CL82 handled attribution correctly.
- Cause: an execution slip despite a clear rule, 「逐個動作保留原文的主詞和對象……合併句子時不得擴大或轉移主詞」. The rule says nothing specific about quoted speech inside a story, which is where this slip happens.
